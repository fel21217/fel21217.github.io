About this Site

I built this site using just HTML and CSS — no frameworks, no drag-and-drop builders, just code, curiosity, and a lot of trial and error. It’s a reflection of how I like to work: hands-on, thoughtful, and always learning.

I created this website because I wanted a space to present my work experience, previous projects, and artwork in a clear and easy-to-access way. Something simple, honest, and focused — where the content speaks for itself.

I chose GitHub Pages to host this site — it’s free, easy to use, and reliable enough to share my work with you!
